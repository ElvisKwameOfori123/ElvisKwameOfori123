# Hi, I'm Elvis Kwame Ofori

**PhD Researcher & Research Assistant · University of Galway (Ryan Institute) · Foresight modelling for agricultural and land-use transitions**

I build models that connect national climate and land-use objectives to the farms, places and
people through which they are carried out. The aim is not to forecast one future, but to explore
plausible pathways before decisions become hard to reverse: **where adjustment falls, who bears
the cost, what opportunities open, and how policy design changes the outcome.** My work sits in
the **FORESIGHT** and **LandingZoNES** research environment and builds on the **GOBLIN** modelling
framework.

## A strategic foresight system for Irish agriculture

Strategic foresight is the ability to perceive, make sense of and act upon different ideas of the
future emerging in the present (OECD, 2021). My models form one such system:

| Role | Model | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Perceive: signals of change | 🔒 GOBLIN-Spatial | How have livestock systems changed in every place, 2015–2025? |
| Perceive: the present, farm by farm | 🔒 AgriSyn v2 | Which farms, of what kind, are where today? |
| Make sense: a shared view | 🔒 GOBLIN-Spatial Explorer | What does that change look like for a place, county or catchment? |
| Act: test instruments | 🔒 StockRate-Sim | How would farms respond to a livestock tax or a tradable cap? |
| Act: stress-test a pathway | 🔒 AgriSyn NZ | Who carries the 2050 net-zero land transition? |
| Learn: a past transition | [E-SEAT](https://github.com/ElvisKwameOfori123/E-SEAT) | Does land use lock in when a demand signal weakens? (US Corn Belt) |

Also public: [BioLand-US](https://github.com/ElvisKwameOfori123/BioLand-US), which asks whether the
land a national bioenergy plan requires can actually be secured and afforded.

🔒 *Private while manuscripts are in preparation or data-release terms are checked. Confidential
microdata are never redistributed.*

## Methods

Synthetic farm populations and mixed-integer optimisation · behavioural microsimulation and
discrete choice · panel econometrics · spatial reconstruction and land allocation · scenario
analysis and stress-testing · Python, Stata, R and GIS, with reproducible pipelines.
